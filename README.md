<a id="overview"></a>
## 🌟 What is All On Deck? (Overview)

Your website is designed with two main parts:

1. **The Public Showcase & Shop:**
   - Shows customers your custom marine EVA foam decking solutions, precision CNC manufacturing, and boat installations.
   - Lets boat owners request free on-site measuring and estimates.
   - Displays color swatches, textures, and completed boat project photos.
   - Allows clients to purchase branded apparel, accessories, and gear with instant checkout.
   - Features an interactive customer assistant ready to answer questions.

2. **The Owner Dashboard:**
   - A private, password-protected control room where you can change website text, upload photos, manage products, view sales reports, track customer orders, and answer quote requests—all without touching any code.

---

<a id="login"></a>
## 🔑 How to Log In as the Owner

1. Open your website in any web browser.
2. Click the **Account / Sign In** button at the top right of the page (or go directly to `/owner`).
3. Enter your **Owner Email and Password**.
4. Once signed in, you will see the **Owner Dashboard** in the navigation bar. Click it to access your management center!

## 🛠️ Managing Your Website (Non-Technical Owner Guide)

Everything on your website can be controlled directly from the sidebar menu inside the **Owner Dashboard**:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         OWNER DASHBOARD MENU                          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  📊 Dashboard Home   - Sales charts, revenue summary & quick stats    |
|  🛠️ Services         - Update your services, descriptions & photos   |
|  🎨 Designs          - Manage foam colors, patterns & materials       |
|  📸 Gallery          - Add and organize photos of completed boats     |
|  🛍️ Products         - Add merch, set prices & manage stock           |
|  📦 Orders           - View paid orders, update shipping & invoices   |
|  👥 Customers        - Customer contact directory & purchase history  |
|  ✉️ Inquiries        - Read new quote requests & contact submissions  |
|  🏷️ Coupons          - Create discount codes (e.g. SUMMER10)          |
|  ⚙️ Settings         - Update phone numbers, emails & store policies  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

<a id="services"></a>
### 1. Services & Offerings

- **What it controls:** The services displayed on the Homepage and `/services` page (e.g., Custom Deck Designs, CNC Manufacturing, Marine Installation).
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Services** in the sidebar.
  - Click **Add Service** or click the **Edit** icon next to an existing one.
  - You can change the title, detailed description, image, and the order they appear on the page.

---

<a id="designs"></a>
### 2. Designs, Colors & Materials

- **What it controls:** The color swatches, router patterns, and material options shown on the `/designs` page.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Designs** in the sidebar.
  - **Colors Tab:** Add new foam color combinations (e.g., Teak / Black, Slate Grey / Orange) and upload swatch preview pictures.
  - **Patterns Tab:** Showcase different CNC routing styles (Hexagon, Diamond, Fish Scale, Classic Planks).

---

<a id="gallery"></a>
### 3. Project Gallery (Completed Boats)

- **What it controls:** The `/gallery` page where potential customers browse your past work.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Gallery** in the sidebar.
  - Click **Upload New Project**.
  - Add the boat make/model (e.g., _Sea Ray 280 Sundancer_), select the category (Center Console, Yacht, Pontoon), and upload crisp photos.
  - You can mark your favorite projects as **Featured** to highlight them on the homepage.

---

<a id="shop"></a>
### 4. Online Shop & Merchandise

- **What it controls:** Hats, shirts, cleaning kits, or accessories sold on `/products`.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Products** in the sidebar.
  - Click **New Product** to set the name, price, description, sizes/colors, and stock count.
  - **Inventory Tracking:** The system automatically reduces stock when someone buys an item and warns you if inventory is running low.

---

<a id="orders"></a>
### 5. Orders, Invoices & Tracking

- **What it controls:** Managing customer purchases made through the online store.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Orders** to see all incoming orders marked as _Pending, Paid, Processing, Shipped, or Delivered_.
  - Click on any order to print a packing slip, view the buyer's shipping address, or paste a **Carrier Tracking Number** (which automatically notifies the buyer).
  - You can also click **Create Manual Order** if a customer pays you over the phone or in person.

---

<a id="estimates"></a>
### 6. Free Estimate Requests & Contact Messages

- **What it controls:** Whenever a boater fills out the **"Get Free Estimate"** form or the footer contact form.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Inquiries / Contact** in the dashboard.
  - You'll see the customer's name, phone number, email, boat model, length, and requested project details.
  - Click to view details and mark them as _New, Contacted, or Scheduled_.

---

<a id="coupons"></a>
### 7. Coupons & Promotions

- **What it controls:** Special discount codes (e.g., `BOATSHOW20` for 20% off or `$50 OFF`).
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Coupons** -> **Create Coupon**.
  - Choose percentage discount or fixed dollar amount, expiration date, and optional minimum order spend.

---

<a id="settings"></a>
### 8. General Settings & Business Info

- **What it controls:** Your business phone number, email, address, Instagram/Facebook links, announcement banner, and return policies.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **Settings** in the dashboard.
  - Edit any text field and click **Save Changes**. The entire website updates immediately.

## 💻 Technical Quick-Start (For Developers & Maintenance)

For developers updating the codebase or deploying changes:

### Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Custom Brand Theme
- **Animations:** Framer Motion 12
- **Database & Auth:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- **Payments:** Stripe Checkout
- **Hosting:** Vercel

### Running Locally

1. Ensure [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (v18+) is installed.
2. Clone the repository and install dependencies:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```
3. Create your `.env` file with your Supabase credentials:
   ```env
   VITE_SUPABASE_URL=your_supabase_url
   VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=your_supabase_anon_key
   ```
4. Start the development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
5. Open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
